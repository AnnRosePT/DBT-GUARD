# Contribution 13: Data-Driven Macro Bottleneck Analytics for SLBC Policy

## Overview
State Level Bankers' Committees (SLBC) and District Collectors rarely receive granular operational insights into why DBT funds bounce.

## Macro Analytics Capabilities
- **Failure Clustering:** Identifies bank branches with abnormally high Aadhaar unmapping rates post-merger.
- **Doorstep IPPB Camp Dispatch:** Pins clusters of unseeded beneficiaries onto a geographic map to schedule mobile postal banking micro-ATMs.
