# Contribution 10: Actionable Pre-Filled Resolution Cards & Banking Mandates

## Overview
Farmers previously visited offices multiple times because they lacked the proper paperwork.

## Features
- **Visual Action Cards:** Clearly state the error in simple terms, the exact office, officer contact, and deadline.
- **Pre-Filled NPCI Annexure-I:** Automatically generates the standard banking mandate form pre-filled with farmer registration details and bank branch data.
