# Contribution 08: Automated SLA Tracking & District Nodal Escalation Matrix

## Overview
Installments left unresolved beyond the 90-day PFMS window risk fund lapse. DBT-GUARD introduces automated SLA tracking.

## SLA Workflow
- **Day 0 - 3 (Green):** Normal resolution window for designated desk.
- **Day 4 - 6 (Amber):** Warning threshold; automated SMS reminder dispatched to officer.
- **Day 7+ (Red / Overdue):** Escalated to the District Nodal Officer's priority intervention dashboard.
