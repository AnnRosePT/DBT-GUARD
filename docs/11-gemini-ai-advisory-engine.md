# Contribution 11: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vernacular Advisory Generation

## Overview
Automated templates often sound robotic or intimidating. DBT-GUARD leverages Google Gemini 2.5 Flash to craft natural, compassionate vernacular scripts.

## Prompting & Architecture
- **Input Parameters:** Farmer name, village panchayat, error type, crop grown, bank branch.
- **Output:** Natural Malayalam tone with step-by-step guidance tailored for verbal reading or SMS readability.
- **Officer Copilot Mode:** Advises Krishi Bhavan desk operators on complex edge cases.
